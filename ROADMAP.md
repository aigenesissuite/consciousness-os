# ROADMAP — What Publishes Next

> **In one sentence:** receipts before rhetoric — the full frontier gate run publishes first, the founder's personal story publishes last, and the order is deliberate.

No dates are promised; artifacts ship when their gates pass. Order is binding: engineering receipts first, philosophy second.

1. **Full gate run** — the 20-case held-out golden set, frontier-class scored model, evidence-cited judging. Publishes to [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md).
2. **Open-source eval harness (MIT)** — case format, runner, judge prompts, aggregation. Run the methodology against any model, including ours.
3. **Marker-geometry study** — whether the seven markers correspond to stable, steerable directions in activation space ([RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) Q4/Q6); the most concrete bridge between the research register and the eval.
4. **Canonical framework docs** — the long-form specification the contract in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) is derived from.
5. **Interactive public demo** — talk to a model holding the contract; try to make it break the bright lines.
6. **Public scoreboard** — frontier models scored against the canonical cases, failures included, third-party reproducible.
7. **Founder essay** — the personal origin story, in the founder's own voice. Downstream of the receipts, deliberately.
8. **Cross-reference series** — where the framework converges with and departs from adjacent work in philosophy of mind and AI alignment.
