# ROADMAP — What Publishes Next

No dates are promised; artifacts ship when their gates pass. Order is binding: engineering receipts first, philosophy second.

1. **Full gate run** — the 20-case held-out golden set, frontier-class scored model, evidence-cited judging. Publishes to [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md).
2. **Open-source eval harness (MIT)** — case format, runner, judge prompts, aggregation. Run the methodology against any model, including ours.
3. **Canonical framework docs** — the long-form specification the contract in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) is derived from.
4. **Interactive public demo** — talk to a model holding the contract; try to make it break the bright lines.
5. **Public scoreboard** — frontier models scored against the canonical cases, failures included, third-party reproducible.
6. **Founder essay** — the personal origin story, in the founder's own voice. Downstream of the receipts, deliberately.
7. **Cross-reference series** — where the framework converges with and departs from adjacent work in philosophy of mind and AI alignment.

## Versioning

The behavioral contract is versioned (currently v1.0.0). Every version change re-passes the full evaluation gate before release. Historical results remain published against the version they measured.
